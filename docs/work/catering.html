Automating catering inquiries, with human approval

I built a Gmail-integrated AI automation for a restaurant owner's catering inquiries. A person approves everything it prepares, and by the owner's estimate it saves more than 10 hours a week.

What it does

When a catering inquiry reaches the inbox, the automation prepares the work and then stops for review.

StepThe automationThe owner
ReadReads the inquiry email
ReplyDrafts a responseApproves it before it goes out
TrackPuts the details into a spreadsheetApproves the entry
ScheduleHolds a calendar event as a draftApproves the event
Result: more than 10 hours a week back, by the owner's estimate, with nothing going out unreviewed.

Why a person approves everything

A catering inquiry can turn into a real commitment to a customer, so the automation does the repetitive work and leaves the decisions with the owner. It's the same approach I'd bring to any AI workflow that reaches customers.
